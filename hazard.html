        Emergency Red Flags — Go To Hospital Immediately

        • Breathing difficulty at rest, or unable to speak a full sentence
        • Bluish lips or fingertips (cyanosis)
        • Chest pain, or heart palpitations that will not settle
        • Confusion, extreme drowsiness, or fainting
        • Infant refusing to feed, or chest wall sucking inward when breathing
        • Asthma reliever inhaler not working after 2 doses

        One-Time Setup Required

        HazeGuard needs a free NASA FIRMS key to detect fire hotspots

        It is 100% free and takes about 2 minutes. NASA gives every user a personal "MAP_KEY" so their servers can manage traffic. Without it, this app can still show PM2.5 and weather, but fire hotspot detection will be disabled — which is the core of smoke source tracking.

        How to get your key

        1. 1
          Click the big orange button below. It opens NASA's official FIRMS Map Key page in a new tab.
        2. 2
          Enter your email address in the form and submit. (No password, no credit card, no account approval wait.)
        3. 3
          NASA instantly displays your MAP_KEY on screen and also emails it to you. It looks like a long string of letters and numbers, e.g. a1b2c3d4e5f6...
        4. 4
          Copy that key, come back to this tab, and paste it into the box below. Then press Save.
        Open NASA FIRMS Key Page Earthdata Sign-Up (optional)

        Your key is stored only in this browser (IndexedDB). It is never sent to any server other than NASA's own API.
