Open Source Intelligence (OSINT) & Radar

A growing radar ecosystem

A portfolio of AI-assisted intelligence systems for discovering weak signals, documenting decentralized knowledge and structuring evidence into research- and policy-ready views.

No login · public demos

GSI Radar

Grassroots Innovation Monitoring

An AI-powered intelligence system that structures decentralized, grassroots innovations into policy-ready insights, including emerging risks and high-impact opportunities.

EDU Radar

Educational Innovation Intelligence

An AI-powered OSINT platform for discovering, analyzing and structuring educational innovations, with Google Scholar and UNESCO UNESDOC integration.

Nexus Radar

Early Warning & Latent Signal Detection

An autonomous AI-powered OSINT engine for detecting creeping environmental and societal anomalies across climate, oceans and biodiversity.

ICH Radar

Cultural Heritage Mapping

An AI-assisted cultural intelligence engine that scans open-web sources to discover, document and structure undocumented Intangible Cultural Heritage practices.

Artifact Radar

Illicit Antiquities Detection

An AI-driven OSINT monitoring system for detecting suspicious online trade patterns, pricing anomalies and missing provenance in antiquities.
